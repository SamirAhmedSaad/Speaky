Now let's take a closer look at the difference between 'every day' and 'everyday'.
'Everyday' is an adjective
that describes something that is normal, common, or routine.
It's used to describe things that are part of our daily lives,
like 'everyday clothes', 'everyday chores', or 'the everyday routine'.
'Every day', on the other hand, is an adverbial phrase
that emphasizes that something happens on a daily basis.
For example,
'I go to the gym every day'
Remember, the adjective 'everyday' usually comes before a noun.
The adverbial phrase 'every day',
on the other hand, is typically seen after a verb.
