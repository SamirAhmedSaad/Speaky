Delete your Speaky account

This page explains how to delete the community account you created in the Speaky app (package com.speaky_ai.app) and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Speaky and tap the chat button on the home screen.
  2. Tap the ⋮ account menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Can't open the app?

Email us from the Google address you signed in with and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your community profile: nickname, gender, level and profile photo.
  • All your chat messages, voice notes, reactions and private conversations.
  • Your blocks, the reports you filed, and your notification tokens.

What we keep

  • Your learning progress, saved words and AI conversations stay on your phone only. They were never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.
